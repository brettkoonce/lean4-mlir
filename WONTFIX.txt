WONTFIX: what this repo has decided not to do, and why. Each entry is a design choice,
a measured dead end, or a limit we accept. Read this before proposing one of them again;
reopening one takes new evidence, not a new argument.

DESIGN

Differentiation is per-layer VJPs, not compiler autodiff
  Every op family carries a hand-written, proved VJP and nets are fixed graphs of them.
  Autodiff of arbitrary Lean code would need a compiler fork (S4TF's fate). A new layer
  type costs one VJP; that is the scope we keep.

jax/ stays; the verified path comes up to meet it
  The JAX trainers are the reference the port is checked against (the VJP oracle, the
  side-by-side tables). IREE could be retired because it was an engine; JAX is the
  oracle. Do not propose deleting jax/ or a reference trainer.

No IEEE model of what XLA computes, and no FloatLib dependency
  rndP (Mathlib only, no axioms) covers every rounding fact a proof uses. No float
  library can speak to the real boundary, XLA/cuDNN reassociation and FMA contraction,
  so a second one would add a require with no consumer. Reopen only if a proof needs
  subnormals or overflow to decide its result.

No bf16 BatchNorm
  Mixed precision as practised: the bf16 flag switches conv, dense, dgrad and wgrad;
  every bf16 net calls the same f32 BN in both branches. A render that cannot reach
  bf16 is on the wrong op family, not missing a BN op. (planning/archive/bf16_batchnorm.md)

No dtype field in the emit IR
  The bf16-boundary project (planning/archive/bf16_dtype_ir.md) was refuted by its own
  test: XLA keeps activations bf16 once the PRODUCING op writes a bf16 result, so the
  fix is a bf16-typed result plus convert, with no dtype in the IR. Convert count is not
  convert cost.

CIFAR BatchNorm stays per-example
  cifar8w_bn reduces over [2,3] and carries no running statistics, so train and eval
  normalize the same way. The batch-coupled BN is a different function.

The book carries results and recipes, not this box
  No stall, throughput-on-this-machine or "add N %" notes in content.tex. Those live in
  the run's RESULTS.md.

Delete, don't archive
  Demos outside the book's table of contents, and ROCm-era experiments, are deleted.
  historical/ holds only the provenance of committed data. Git history is the archive.

main moves by fast-forward only
  No merge commits on main.

A run is a dated directory
  runs/YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>/. Suite and gate output (runs/platform/) commits its summary,
  not its logs. scripts/gates/repo_shape.txt is the allow list; the user changes it.

MEASURED DEAD ENDS

bf16 does not pay at CIFAR shapes
  At at most 32 channels the convs are launch-bound; bf16 was slower than f32 over
  cifar8's whole conv stack. CIFAR bf16 is a stability result, not a speed one.

The BN statistic group cannot be measured on Imagenette
  R50 on Imagenette overfits until BN noise is the only regulariser, and the probe's sign
  came out opposite to the ImageNet pairs. The ImageNet sync-BN pairs in the book are the
  evidence. (planning/global_bn_verified.md, jax/scripts/bn_sharding_demo.py)

LIMITS

The generic spec walk (LeanMlir/MlirCodegen.lean) has no bf16
  The bf16 and bf16Conv config fields are read only by the render emitters and the JAX
  codegen; the demos on the generic walk train f32 whatever the flag says. Giving them
  bf16 means porting the op twins, a project, not a config change.
