Reading: the worst slices are the lower chest and shoulders of large patients, where Mayo's insertion puts 300–800 HU
of noise — far outside the 10% build's training range (median added sigma 117 HU) — and there the ancestral DDIM
sampler diverges (|sample| in the tens of thousands of HU). The MSE net degrades gracefully on the same slices
(514 at worst). Where the noise is within range the generator works (median slice 202 -> 132 HU). Two mitigations,
neither applied here: clamp the sample to the HU range each step (bounds, does not fix, the tail), or train across
a wider dose range. The 25%-trained generator on the same slices does not diverge (its sample stays near the floor,
median ratio ~0.97) — it draws residuals at its own 62 HU scale and leaves the rest.
