# proof_cleanup.md — proof-quality and compile-time cleanup

Started 2026-09-22 from a six-way read of every hand-written proof file against
`proof_cleanup_audits/rubric.md` (brittle chains, undocumented defeq, heartbeat bumps, long
proofs, repetition), plus a per-module build-time map pooled from ~170 CI runs. The six reports
(`proof_cleanup_audits/audit_*.md`) quote code at file:line; they are static reads — nothing in
them was compiled, so every "saves X s" in them is a prediction until measured. Items below that
were measured say so.

## 0. Rules for this thread

* Measure before and after, on one module, with `lake env lean <file>` or `lake build <module>`;
  a scratch copy outside `LeanMlir/` (e.g. the session scratchpad) compiles standalone without
  shadowing the real module.
* A bump's comment records why it was needed once, not whether it still is — strip it and
  compile (§1b found 57 of 59 dead).
* Generated files (`Generated by` / `GENERATED by` headers — both casings exist) are changed in
  the generator, then regenerated; confirm the unedited generator is byte-reproducible first.
* Whole-net ties peel with `rw`, never `simp only` over `rfl` lemmas (the kernel re-derives the
  step, 48 GB on 4.34); collapse lemmas are stated at variable shapes, never at the net's
  numerals.
* Lemmas for `Foundation/Tensor.lean`, `Codegen/StableHLO.lean` or `Codegen/Lamb.lean` rebuild
  240–420 modules: park them in the leaf that needs them and move them as a batch.

## 3. Next, in order

### 3.1 ResNet-34 apex re-spelling (the one slow hand-written tie)

`r34InputGradB_eq_r34B_full_vjp` closes with one `rfl` through seventeen `vjp_comp_diff_at`s and
keeps `maxRecDepth 800000` / `maxHeartbeats 1000000` (the only hand-written bumps left in Nets).
ConvNeXt's fix (`vjp_comp_diff_at_fst_backward`, `ConvNeXtWholeBackCertifiedTieB.lean:398`,
peeled by `repeat rw`) does NOT port as-is — tried 2026-09-22 on a scratch copy:

* nested apex term (no `let`s) + `repeat rw [peel]`: the rewrite never matches. Each witness
  `hbₖ` is typed at `opaqueA(k-1) … x` (a plain `def`), the apex's inner map is the spelled chain
  `bₖ₋₁ ∘ … ∘ stem`, and `rw`'s keyed matching only unfolds reducible constants;
* `erw`: max recursion depth unifying the two spellings (the header's "two spellings" wall);
* `attribute [local reducible] opaqueA*`: rejected by 4.34 for another module's defs.

What ConvNeXt has and R34 lacks: saved prefixes as point-free `abbrev`s that are BOTH the apex's
inner maps and the witnesses' points. Plan: define `r34SavedB_k` abbrevs (point-free,
`bₖ ∘ r34SavedB_{k-1}`), restate `r34B_full_has_vjp_at`'s witnesses at `r34SavedB_{k-1} … x`,
nest the apex, peel with `rw`, drop both bumps. Ripple: the tie's statement (witness types) and
its consumers in Nets/ResNet; R50 reuses the stem/head. It also retires the second prefix
vocabulary (`r34PreK` + `_apply`, 34 decls) — audit_resnet_small_convnext.md findings 1 and 3.
Move the peel lemma and `vjp_comp_diff_at` itself (now in per-net `ResNet34.lean`) to
`Foundation/OpaquePrefix.lean` in the same change.

## 4. Checked and set aside

* The audit's estimate that `binomTailNum` was quadratic-dominated was right in kind, wrong in
  scale (it projected ~55 s from the worst entry; the module's kernel time was 93 s locally).
* `FloatBridge`'s `nlinarith` → `linarith` is idiom, not speed (−2 s).
* A plain (non-tail) recursive loop for the binomial tail hits `(kernel) deep recursion` at 4,835
  steps; so does a `Nat.rec` fold. Tail-recursive with accumulator arguments is what works.
